Return After Your Trek
Return all gear to your guide on your last day in Kathmandu. Simple, no hassle, no shipping.
Pro Tip: Travel Light, Trek Right
Most trekkers from outside Asia pay $50–$150 in extra baggage fees for bulky gear they only use once. Renting in Kathmandu saves money, saves luggage space, and you get tried-and-tested gear that's known to work at altitude. The quality of rental gear available in Thamel is excellent — most is the same brand gear sold in Western stores.
