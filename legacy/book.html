🏔️ Best Times to Trek the Khumbu
Autumn (Oct–Nov): Crystal-clear skies, stable weather, crisp air — the most popular season.
Spring (Mar–May): Rhododendrons blooming, slightly warmer, also excellent — Everest climbing season.
Winter (Dec–Feb): Fewer trekkers, cold (-20°C possible), some high passes closed. EBC still possible.
Monsoon (Jun–Sep): Lush greenery, dramatic skies, significant rain. Possible but requires flexibility.
