Your Guide

Born in the Shadow of Everest

Peak Pursuit was founded by a lifelong Sherpa guide who grew up in the Khumbu Valley with Everest as his backyard. Having led over 500 expeditions and with a decade-plus of guiding experience, he combines deep mountain knowledge with an infectious warmth that makes every trek unforgettable.

Fluent in Nepali, Sherpa, English, and Hindi, and certified by Nepal Mountaineering Association, he doesn't just show you the mountains — he shares the stories, culture, and spirit of the Himalayas that most trekkers never get to experience.

NMA Certified Guide
Nepal Mountaineering Association certified, with advanced wilderness first aid training and high-altitude rescue qualifications.
Local Expert Knowledge
Born and raised in the Khumbu region. Knows every hidden teahouse, shortcut, and secret viewpoint that guidebooks don't mention.
Small Group Focus
Maximum 8 guests per group, ensuring personal attention, flexibility, and an authentic experience — not a conveyor belt tour.
